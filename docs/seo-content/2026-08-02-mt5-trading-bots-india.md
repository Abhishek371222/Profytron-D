# MT5 Trading Bots in India: How to Deploy Automated Strategies Safely

> Live site copy also at `/blog/mt5-trading-bots-india` after deploy (in `apps/web/src/lib/blog/posts.ts`).

Automated trading is no longer exclusive to funds. If you can use MetaTrader 5, you can run bots that place and manage trades while you work — if you treat risk as a product feature.

## What an MT5 trading bot does

Software follows rules (entries, exits, size) and sends orders through **your** broker. On Profytron, capital stays in your MT4/MT5 account. The platform executes; it does not custody funds.

## Paper first

Confirm behavior, risk limits, and pacing with paper trading before live MT5.

## Pick marketplace bots carefully

Track record length, max drawdown, sessions/symbols, and true broker-fed verification (60+ days for Verified on Profytron).

## Risk limits before first live fill

Set account drawdown limits first. Automated pause is most useful when you would be least rational.
