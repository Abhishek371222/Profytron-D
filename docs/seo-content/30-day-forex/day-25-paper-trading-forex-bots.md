## Why paper

Observe fills, pacing, and emotional urge to intervene without ruining capital.

## Demo is imperfect

Demo liquidity is not always identical to live. Still far superior to going cold into real size.

## Profytron path

Start free paper flows when available; only later connect live MT5.

## Try this

Define a paper gate: e.g. two weeks behaviour match vs description before live.
