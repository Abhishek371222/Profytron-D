# What Is a Forex Trading Bot (EA)?

<!-- internal: series day 22/30 suggest 2026-08-24 -->

## Definition

A forex trading bot (often an Expert Advisor on MT4/MT5) is software that places and manages orders from coded rules without needing you to click every entry.

## What bots are good at

Consistency of execution, multi-session presence with VPS, emotionless rule following — **if** rules and risk are sound.

## What bots are bad at

Magic profit guarantees, adapting silently to regime shifts without engineered filters, and forgiving toxic brokerage conditions.

## Profytron stance

Bots execute; capital stays at your broker; risk limits should sit above strategies.
