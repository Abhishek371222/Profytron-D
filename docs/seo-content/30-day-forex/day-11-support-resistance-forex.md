# Support and Resistance on Currency Charts

<!-- internal: series day 11/30 suggest 2026-08-13 -->

## Levels are zones

Treat as areas, not single-tick magic prices. Prior swing highs/lows, session opens, and obvious consolidations form often-watched zones.

## Break vs fakeout

Forex loves liquidity runs beyond obvious levels then reversals. Stops clustered beyond levels are a feature of the market, not a conspiracy theory you must personally defeat.

## Risk placement

Invalidation should live where your idea is objectively wrong — not 'two pips of hope'.
