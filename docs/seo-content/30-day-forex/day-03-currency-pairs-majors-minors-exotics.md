# Majors, Minors, and Exotics Explained

<!-- internal: series day 3/30 suggest 2026-08-05 -->

## Majors

Majors usually include the most liquid USD pairs: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, AUDUSD, USDCAD, NZDUSD. Tighter spreads, denser order books, fewer 'surprise gaps' relative to thin markets — not zero risk.

## Crosses / minors

Pairs without USD as a leg, e.g. EURJPY, EURGBP. Still heavily traded, but costs and behaviour can differ from majors.

## Exotics

Emerging or low-liquidity pairs can show wide spreads, sudden spikes, and stop hunts. Fine for specialists; dangerous as a beginner lab.
