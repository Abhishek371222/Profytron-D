# Trend Following Basics in Forex

<!-- internal: series day 12/30 suggest 2026-08-14 -->

## Trend definition you can test

Higher highs/higher lows (up) vs lower highs/lower lows (down) on a chosen timeframe. If you cannot define it, you cannot automate it.

## Moving averages as filters — not oracles

Averages lag. They can reduce noise entries; they never 'predict the top'.

## When trend systems fail

Range-bound FX regimes, news shocks, and overnight gaps. Expect losing periods.

## Try this

Write a 5-line rule: trend definition, entry, stop, target or trail, do-not-trade hours. You are one step from a bot spec.
