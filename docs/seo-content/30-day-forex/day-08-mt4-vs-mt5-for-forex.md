# MT4 vs MT5 for Forex Operators

<!-- internal: series day 8/30 suggest 2026-08-10 -->

## Why MetaTrader dominates automated FX

MT4 and MT5 are standard at many forex CFDs brokers, with large ecosystems of Expert Advisors (EAs), indicators, and history tools.

## MT4 strengths

Mature EA ecosystem, still extremely common for FX automation that already 'works'.

## MT5 strengths

Richer instrument model and more capable testing tools for many workflows.
