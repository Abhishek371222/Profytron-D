# Backtesting Forex Bots Without Fooling Yourself

<!-- internal: series day 24/30 suggest 2026-08-26 -->

## Purpose

Backtests explore behaviour; they do not mint truth. Costs, spreads, and data quality dominate.

## Look-ahead and overfitting

If parameters only work after endless optimization on one sample, live bleed is likely.

## Walk-forward / out-of-sample

Hold out recent data. Require stability across periods before size.

## Try this

When evaluating any bot claim, demand sample length + max DD + cost assumptions.
