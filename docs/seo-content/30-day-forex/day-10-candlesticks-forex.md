# Candlesticks for Forex: Reading Price Action Without Mystery

<!-- internal: series day 10/30 suggest 2026-08-12 -->

## OHLC meaning

Open, high, low, close compress auction results. Body and wick tell who controlled the bar — buyers or sellers — but context (level + session) matters more than pattern names collected from lists.

## Useful patterns = useful only near levels

A hammer in the middle of nowhere is noise. A rejection at a pre-planned level is information.

## For bots

Many bots encode candle mathematics without naming patterns. You still need to know what kind of market structure they thrash in.

## Try this

On H1 of your major, mark three bars that rejected a clear swing level. Ignore pattern branding; write what the auction did.
