# Position Sizing That Keeps You Alive

<!-- internal: series day 16/30 suggest 2026-08-18 -->

## Fixed fractional thinking

Risking ~0.25–1% per idea is a common survival band — not a law of nature. Higher rates demand extremely robust edges and psychology.

## Distance of invalidation drives size

Wide stops → smaller size. Tight stops → still not unlimited size; spread and noise matter.

## Bots must encode size

If size is 'whatever the provider defaulted', you inherited someone else's risk tolerance.
