# What Is Forex? The Market Behind Currency Prices

<!-- internal: series day 1/30 suggest 2026-08-03 -->

## The simple definition

Forex (foreign exchange) is the global market where currencies are traded in pairs — for example EUR/USD (euro versus US dollar). When you buy EUR/USD you are effectively long euros and short dollars. Prices move when supply and demand for one currency relative to another change.

Unlike a single stock exchange that has a hard close bell for most of the day, forex is a network of banks, brokers, funds, and retail platforms that quote almost around the clock from Monday open in Asia through late Friday New York.

## Why pairs — not 'buy euro alone'

Currencies have relative value. There is no useful 'price of the dollar in isolation' for trading. Everything is expressed as how many units of the quote currency buy one unit of the base currency. That structure is permanent — even when your platform shows a clean buy/sell button.

## Who trades this market

Participants include central banks, commercial banks, hedge funds, corporations hedging payables, market makers, and individual operators. You do not need to out-guess all of them. You need a process: defined risk, defined edge theory, and honest measurement.
