# Pips, Lots, Leverage, and Margin

<!-- internal: series day 4/30 suggest 2026-08-06 -->

## Pip basics

A pip is the standard unit of price move for most FX pairs (often 0.0001 for XXXX.XXXX quotes; JPY pairs use 0.01). Your P&L depends on pip value × size.

## Lot sizes

Standard lot ≈ 100,000 base units; mini 10,000; micro 1,000 on many brokers. Smaller size is not weakness — it is survival.

## Leverage is a loan against the notionals

1:100 leverage does **not** mean '100× more skill'. It means small capital can control large notional exposure — which multiplies **losses** as fast as gains. Broker margin rules can liquidate you.

## Risk formula mindset

Decide dollars (or account %) you can lose per trade first. **Then** compute size. Never size from 'lots everyone uses on YouTube'.

## Try this

If risk per idea = 0.5% of account and stop is 30 pips, estimate rough size conceptually (even as a sketch). If you can't write the steps, do not go live.
