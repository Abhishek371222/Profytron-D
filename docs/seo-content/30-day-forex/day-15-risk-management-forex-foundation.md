## Risk first, edge second

A mediocre edge with excellent risk can survive. A 'great' edge with terrible risk is a delayed wipeout.

## Pre-commitment

Write max risk per trade and max daily/weekly loss before the session. After three losses, revenge trading rewrites the rules unless automation enforces them.

## Profytron AI risk framing

Account-level risk engines exist to pause bots when your configured drawdown is breached — protection against correlated bot stacking as much as against one bad entry.

## Try this

Set: max risk per idea, max daily stop-out, max concurrent bots once you automate. Pin the paper near the monitor.
