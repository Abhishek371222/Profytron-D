# Spreads, Slippage, and Real Trading Costs

<!-- internal: series day 5/30 suggest 2026-08-07 -->

## Spread

The difference between bid and ask is the first cost of every round-turn. Scalpers die by spread. Swing strategies feel it less — until they overtrade.

## Commission vs raw spreads

Some ECN-style accounts charge commission with razor spreads; others embed cost in spread. Compare **all-in** cost, not marketing labels.

## Slippage

You request a price; you may fill worse — especially around news and thin books. Bots are not immune; they can be more exposed if they spam orders at bad moments.

## Try this

Watch your broker’s spread on your chosen pair at two times: liquid overlap and late thin hours. Write the difference. That delta is a forced filter for many bots.
