# Process Over Emotion (Even When a Bot Is On)

<!-- internal: series day 20/30 suggest 2026-08-22 -->

## Automation is not emotion-proof

Humans still toggle bots off after two losses or crank risk after three wins. That UI click is psychology.

## Rules for interventions

Define when you are allowed to pause a bot: risk breach, technical outage, defined regime filter — not 'I feel bad'.
