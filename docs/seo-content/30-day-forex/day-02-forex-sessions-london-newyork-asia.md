# Forex Sessions: Asia, London, New York

<!-- internal: series day 2/30 suggest 2026-08-04 -->

## Why sessions matter

Liquidity and volatility cluster when major centres overlap. Spreads can tighten in liquid hours and widen in thin ones. A bot that works in European morning may behave differently into the American close.

## Rough map (local times vary with DST)

**Asia:** Tokyo/Sydney contribute early volume.
**London:** often the liquidity core for EUR, GBP, and many crosses.
**New York:** USD-heavy flow; London–New York overlap is often the most active window.

Weekend gap risk exists because the market pauses and reopens.

## Strategy implication

Session filters are real edge tools. Trading news spikes or ultra-thin Asian hours without a plan is a common account killer for both humans and poorly filtered bots.

## Try this

Note your local wake hours vs London overlap. Decide which 4-hour window you will study this week — and which hours your future bot may be **blocked** from trading.
