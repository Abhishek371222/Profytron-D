# Going Long and Short in Forex

<!-- internal: series day 6/30 suggest 2026-08-08 -->

## Long / short mapped to pairs

Buying EURUSD = bullish euro vs dollar. Selling EURUSD = the reverse. There is no need for a special shorting mechanics lecture like hard-to-borrow stocks; the pair structure embeds both sides.

## Bias is not a strategy

A thesis ('USD weak next month') still needs timing, risk, and invalidation. Opinion without stops is gambling with extra vocabulary.

## Bots encode both sides

Automated systems can flip long/short by rule. Your job is to understand the rule family, not to babysit each tick.

## Try this

On a demo chart, mark one historical long and one short that would have respected a simple swing structure. Note where a stop belonged.
