# Mean Reversion vs Breakout Logic

<!-- internal: series day 13/30 suggest 2026-08-15 -->

## Two families of edge

Breakout systems bet on expansion after compression. Mean reversion bets on return toward value after excess. Mixing them without rules creates self-canceling behaviour.

## Costs hunt the wrong family

High-frequency mean-reversion ideas die under spreads. Patient breakouts die under false breaks. Know your cost footprint.

## Bot shops often mix labels

Read marketplace descriptions for *logic family*, not for adjectives like 'AI-powered' alone.

## Try this

Classify one public strategy description as breakout, reversion, or unclear. If unclear, that is a no-deploy.
