# Drawdown: Peak-to-Trough Reality

<!-- internal: series day 17/30 suggest 2026-08-19 -->

## Definition

Drawdown measures decline from a prior equity peak. Live traders quit strategies on max pain, not on backtest average.

## Why max DD marketing is incomplete

Sample length, regime, and strategy changes matter. A 8% max DD on 30 days of trending gold is not a lifetime guarantee.

## Survive to compound

Avoid strategies that require drawdowns larger than you will honestly tolerate.

## Try this

Write your personal max acceptable DD as a percentage. Any bot claiming multiples of that as 'normal' is not for you.
